Three things worth knowing

  1. First Class is almost never on time.

    95.3% of First Class shipments are late. They're promised in 1 day and take 2, every time.

  2. Nearly one in five orders loses money.

    12,347 orders ended up in the red, mostly from discounts above 20% on thin-margin items like cleats and footwear.

  3. Europe and Latam bring in most of the revenue.

    Together they account for 57.5% of sales ($10.87M and $10.28M). Pacific Asia is smaller but has the best margin at 4.42%.

Big sellers, small margins

60%+ of revenue comes from just three categories: Fishing, Cleats and Women's Apparel.

Those big sellers run on margins of about 2.3–3.4%. Some small categories, like Golf Bags & Carts (15.95%) and Toys (14.75%), earn four times that, but together they make up less than 0.1% of sales.

What I'd try: put a 3% minimum margin on Cleats and Men's Footwear, and stop discount coupons from stacking on them.
