Volume vs Margin Paradox

60%+ of revenue generated by Fishing, Cleats, Apparel

The highest-volume categories run on narrow 2.3% – 3.4% margins. Conversely, niche categories like Golf Bags & Carts (15.95%) and Toys (14.75%) enjoy 4× the margin but represent less than 0.1% of sales.

Strategic Recommendation: Institute a minimum 3% gross margin floor on Cleats and Men's Footwear by restricting stackable discount coupons.
